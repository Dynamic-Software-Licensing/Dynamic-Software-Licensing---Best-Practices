# 02. Scope

## Chapter Status

- Status: Working Draft
- Editorial maturity: outline
- Normative readiness: not started
- Open drafting points: yes

## Purpose

This chapter defines the scope boundaries of the Dynamic Software Licensing best practices.

It clarifies what the best practices covers, what it intentionally excludes, and who it is written for. Its role is to keep the document focused and to help contributors decide whether a topic belongs in the main best practices, in an annex, or outside the best practices altogether.

## Scope of the DSL Best Practices

The Dynamic Software Licensing best practices are intended to address modern software licensing approaches for broadcast and media workflows.

The main focus is on reusable and transferable concepts that can support industry alignment across different vendors, deployment models, and operational environments.

## In Scope

Topics that are expected to be in scope include:
- software licensing approaches for broadcast and media workflows,
- software-based, hybrid, and software-defined licensing concepts,
- entitlement, lease, renewal, move, revoke, and usage-recording concepts,
- API-oriented licensing interactions,
- workload identity-related binding concepts,
- metering, usage tracking, and policy handling,
- observability, auditability, and operational transparency,
- migration from legacy licensing approaches toward more modern target models.

## Out of Scope

Topics that are expected to remain out of scope include:
- the media services themselves,
- detailed ERP, CRM, or finance and billing system integration models,
- legal contract wording,
- commercial price lists,
- vendor-specific product packaging models,
- formal certification procedures,
- platform-specific implementation detail unless explicitly included as informative reference material.

These topics may be referenced where useful, but they are not the primary subject of the best practices.

## Intended Audience

This best-practices document is intended for:
- manufacturers / ISVs,
- broadcasters and media organizations,
- system integrators,
- architects and platform designers,
- operations and engineering teams,
- finance and procurement stakeholders,
- security and compliance stakeholders.

## Main Document Boundary

The main best practices should remain focused on platform-agnostic concepts wherever possible.

Where material becomes:
- platform-specific,
- deployment-specific,
- vendor-specific,
- example-driven rather than generally applicable,

it should normally be placed in an annex rather than the core best practices.

## Role of This Chapter

This chapter should help contributors answer the following questions:
- Does this topic belong in the main DSL best practices?
- Should this content be moved into a normative annex?
- Should this content be moved into an informative annex?
- Is this topic outside the intended scope of the best practices?

## Possible Future Expansion

If needed, this chapter may later be expanded into subchapters such as:
- in-scope topics,
- out-of-scope topics,
- intended audience,
- scope boundary principles,
- relationship to annex material.

## Drafting Note

At the current stage, this chapter is primarily intended to establish scope discipline.

Its purpose is to prevent the best practices from becoming too broad, too vendor-specific, or too closely tied to implementation detail that belongs elsewhere in the repository.
