# 01. Industry Context

## Purpose

This subchapter describes the broader industry transformation that forms the backdrop for the Dynamic Software Licensing initiative.

It explains the shift from hardware-bound, capital-expenditure-driven operating models toward software-defined, cloud-enabled, and hybrid production environments, and why that shift has direct consequences for how software is licensed.
