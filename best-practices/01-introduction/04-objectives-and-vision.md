# 04. Objectives and Vision

## Purpose

This subchapter defines what the DSL best practices are intended to achieve and describes the target vision for a modern, cloud-ready licensing model.

It outlines the core principles — API-first, serverless, security by design, observability, and licensing-as-code — that underpin the entire framework, and explains the strategic direction the industry is moving toward.
