# 05. Workflow Definitions

## Purpose

This section captures canonical workflow definitions that are used to derive system requirements from challenges and use-case analysis.

## Scope

Workflow definitions should include:
- workflow trigger and objective,
- actors and responsibilities,
- preconditions and required inputs,
- step sequence and decision points,
- expected outputs and state changes,
- failure paths and recovery handling,
- traceability links to derived requirement themes.

## Drafting Guidance

Workflow definitions should remain platform-agnostic and implementation-oriented.

They should explicitly support derivation into Chapter 09 requirement statements.
